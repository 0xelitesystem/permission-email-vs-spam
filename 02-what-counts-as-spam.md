# What counts as spam

In practice, spam is any commercial email sent to someone who did not ask for it and does not want it. The legal definitions vary, but the working definition that protects your business is simpler: if the recipient did not opt in, treat it as spam and do not send it.

## The recipient decides

Mailbox providers judge spam largely by how recipients react. If people delete without opening, mark as spam, or never engage, the provider learns that your mail is unwanted and starts routing it to the spam folder, for everyone. You do not get to declare your mail legitimate; the inbox decides based on behavior. This is why permission matters even beyond the law: it produces the engagement that keeps you in the inbox.

## Beyond the legal minimum

Some email can be technically legal in a given jurisdiction and still function as spam, in the sense that recipients did not want it and providers will penalize it. Meeting the legal requirements is necessary but not sufficient. The goal is mail people are glad to receive, which is a higher bar than mail that is merely lawful.

## Common spam signals

Mail tends to be treated as spam when it goes to addresses that never opted in, when it hides or fakes who it is from, when it lacks a working way to unsubscribe, when the subject line misrepresents the content, or when it is sent at volume to disengaged recipients. Each of these both annoys people and trips provider filters.

## The cost of being wrong

Being labeled a spammer is not a warning; it is a deliverability problem that follows your domain and sending address. Once providers distrust your mail, even your wanted messages struggle to land. Rebuilding sender reputation is slow. This is why the safe default is strict: when in doubt about whether someone wanted your email, do not send it.

## The simple test

Before sending, ask whether this specific recipient asked to hear from you about this kind of thing. If yes, proceed. If no, or if you are not sure, that is your answer. The discipline of only mailing people who opted in is what keeps you out of the spam category entirely.
