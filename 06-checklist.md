# Pre-send checklist

Run this before any commercial email send. It is built around the permission-first approach, which keeps you both effective and inside the rules.

## Permission

- [ ] Every recipient opted in to this kind of email.
- [ ] No bought, rented, or scraped addresses are on the list.
- [ ] The list reflects the expectations you set at signup.

## Identity and honesty

- [ ] The from name and address clearly identify you or your business.
- [ ] Header and routing information is truthful, not disguised.
- [ ] The subject line accurately reflects the content.
- [ ] A valid physical postal address is included where required.

## Opt-out

- [ ] A clear, working unsubscribe link is present.
- [ ] Unsubscribe requests are honored promptly and permanently.
- [ ] Previous opt-outs have already been removed from this send.

## List health

- [ ] Hard-bounced addresses have been removed.
- [ ] Long-unengaged subscribers have been re-engaged or pruned.
- [ ] The complaint rate from recent sends is low.

## Content and promise

- [ ] The frequency and topic match what subscribers agreed to.
- [ ] The message offers value, not just an ask.

## Legal

- [ ] You have confirmed the rules that apply to your recipients' jurisdictions.
- [ ] Anything carrying legal weight has been checked with a qualified professional.

## The one-line version

If everyone on this list asked to hear from you, you are honest about who you are, and leaving is easy, you are almost certainly fine. If any of those is not true, fix that before you send.
